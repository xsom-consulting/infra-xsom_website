Expertise

From IT strategy to live production

Three complementary practices, available separately or together. On complex programmes, what makes the difference is precisely the ability to hold infrastructure, security and data within a single line of reasoning.

Practice 01

Telecom, network & cybersecurity

Our founding ground. Eighteen years designing, operating and securing carrier networks and industrial infrastructure — environments where an outage is measured in real losses, not satisfaction points.

That experience lets us run compliance programmes without reducing them to a documentation exercise: we know what a recovery plan actually involves on the day it is triggered.

Network architecture & governance

Flow mapping, target architecture, enterprise architecture planning, migration paths and high-bandwidth infrastructure.

Compliance programmes

Regulatory programme leadership: DORA for financial services, NIS2, French critical infrastructure law, and sector-specific frameworks.

Continuity & resilience

Business continuity and disaster recovery planning, failover testing, protection of critical operations.

Audit & decommissioning

Infrastructure audits, obsolescence management, data centre relocation and critical system migration.

Practice 02

AI infrastructure & sovereign cloud

Behind every model there is infrastructure: servers that draw power, networks that carry traffic, data centres that generate heat, regulations that keep moving. It is the layer nobody talks about and everything depends on.

We apply to AI the same rigour we apply to carrier networks — because the promise of artificial intelligence only materialises if the foundations hold.

See the AI & sovereignty offering in detail

Sovereign architectures

Data residency strategy, certified sovereign hosting, private cloud, on-premise and hybrid architectures for intensive training workloads.

Data centre & Green IT

GPU capacity planning, energy optimisation, cooling strategy and reduction of the overall footprint.

Observability & FinOps

Unified monitoring across application and infrastructure layers, GPU cost control, AIOps practices.

EU AI Act compliance

System classification, regulatory documentation, algorithmic governance and independent audit of production pipelines.

Practice 03

Data science, ML & automation

A model that never reaches production creates no value. Our commitment covers the entire chain — from data ingestion through to retraining under real operating conditions.

We do not deliver proofs of concept. We deliver systems your teams run, monitor and evolve without us.

Data engineering

Multi-source ingestion, normalisation, qualification, deduplication, enrichment and cataloguing.

Modelling

Supervised and unsupervised learning, feature engineering, anomaly detection, predictive scoring.

MLOps & industrialisation

Scoring APIs, integration into business processes, model CI/CD, drift monitoring and retraining.

Data governance

End-to-end lineage, access management, GDPR compliance, documentation of algorithmic decisions.
