Cookie policy

This site sets no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. No consent banner is therefore required.

This page is a translation provided for convenience. The French version is the legally binding one.

What we do not do

  • No advertising cookiesWe run no advertising and sell no browsing data.
  • No analytics trackersNeither Google Analytics nor any equivalent. We do not follow your path through the site.
  • No social network pixelsLinks to LinkedIn are plain outbound links, with no embedded tracking script.

Third-party services

Fonts Typefaces are served from this domain, not from an external content delivery network. No request leaves your browser to load them, and no IP address is shared.
Web3Forms Delivers messages sent through the contact form. Contacted only at the moment you submit it, and only then. No cookie is set.
GitHub Pages Hosts the site. The host may keep technical connection logs for security and availability purposes.
Local preferences Theme and reduced-motion choices are saved in your browser’s local storage. Sound starts off on each visit. No tracking identifier is stored.

Controlling your browsing

Nothing is required on your side to browse this site without being tracked. Should you wish to strengthen your privacy more generally, every browser lets you block third-party cookies and requests to external domains from its settings.
