Who we look for

Curious. Self-directed. Committed.

We are not looking for people who execute instructions. We are looking for consultants who ask the right questions, who work well with client teams and who want to understand the business stakes behind the technical work.

Years of experience matter less than rigour, curiosity and the desire to do things properly.
